What Happens in an SEO Consultation

A first SEO Consultation is a short video call to understand your business, goals, market and current site.

I will ask about your main services or products, where your customers come from today, any past SEO work and what success would look like.

Where possible, I review your site beforehand so the call can focus on specific opportunities rather than generalities.

After the call, you receive a summary of what I see as the main priorities and, if it makes sense to work together, a scoped proposal with timeline and price.

How to Prepare

  • Your website address and any other domains you use

  • Your main services, products and target locations

  • Access to Google Search Console or analytics if available (read-only is enough)

  • Any recent changes, such as a redesign or traffic drop

  • Your budget range and timeline
