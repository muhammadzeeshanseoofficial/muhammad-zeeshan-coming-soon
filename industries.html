Why industry SEO beats generic SEO

A roofer, a florist and a SaaS company all need SEO, but their customers behave differently.

Roofing searches are urgent and local after storms; florist searches spike before Valentine's Day and Mother's Day; B2B buyers research for weeks and compare vendors. Industry SEO adapts keyword research, page structure, content and local signals to those patterns, so effort goes where it pays back.

Each industry page explains the searches that matter, the pages a business in that sector needs, a 90-day plan, how things differ across the US, UK, Canada, Australia and the UAE, and how results are measured.
