Marketing agency SEO that brings your agency inbound clients

Marketing agency SEO helps digital, creative and performance agencies rank for the services and niches they sell, and turns their own website into a steady source of client enquiries.

Get a free SEO auditBook a call

Why agencies struggle with their own SEO

Agencies often do excellent work for clients but neglect their own sites. Service pages are generic, case studies sit in PDFs, the blog stopped months ago and the site is redesigned every couple of years without redirects. Marketing agency SEO fixes that with a clear positioning, focused service and niche pages, real case studies and content that shows how the agency thinks.

Agency searches are competitive for broad terms like digital marketing agency, but much less so for specific combinations: PPC agency for e-commerce, B2B content agency, marketing agency for dentists or Shopify agency [city]. Niche and service-plus-niche pages are where most agencies win.

Local searches still matter for agencies that serve their city, so a Google Business Profile optimization with reviews from clients helps.

If you want to resell SEO to your clients under your own brand rather than rank your own agency, see white label SEO.

Ranking is only half the job; the page also has to turn a visitor into an enquiry. Every key page I build for this industry includes:

A 90-day marketing agency seo plan

  1. Positioning. Choose core services and niches.
  2. Service and niche pages. Detailed, specific pages.
  3. Case studies. HTML pages with approved results.
  4. Thought leadership. Opinionated, useful content.
  5. Profile and reviews. Local visibility and client reviews.

Marketing agency seo across the US, UK, Canada, Australia and the UAE

UK agencies compete for searches such as digital marketing agency London or Manchester; US agencies by city and niche; Australian agencies in Sydney and Melbourne; UAE agencies in Dubai with multilingual audiences. Search terms such as agency versus company vary by market.

How results are measured

Track qualified enquiries, proposal rate and new client revenue from organic search, plus rankings for target service-niche terms. Ask every new client how they found you, and compare that with analytics.

Case studies that rank and sell

Agency case studies usually sit in sales decks. Turning them into web pages with the client's industry, the challenge, the approach, the results the client approved and a quote gives you unique content, ranks for niche searches and helps prospects decide. Link each case study to the relevant service and niche pages.

Avoiding redesign traffic losses

Agencies redesign their own sites often and sometimes lose years of rankings through changed URLs without redirects. Before any redesign, map old URLs to new ones, keep high-value pages and test thoroughly. It is the same discipline you would apply to a client site.

Thought leadership with a point of view

Generic marketing tips are everywhere. Agencies stand out with opinions, frameworks and data from their own work. Fewer, better pieces that reflect how your team actually thinks attract both prospects and links, and are more likely to be cited by AI assistants.

Original research is especially effective. An annual survey of clients in your niche, benchmarks drawn from anonymised campaign data or a teardown of common mistakes gives journalists, bloggers and AI systems something worth citing, and it positions your agency as the expert rather than one of many.

Team pages and expertise

Prospects want to know who will work on their account. Team pages with experience and specialisms add trust and give search engines clearer signals about your expertise.

Short bios with each person's background, certifications and the kinds of clients they work with help prospects picture the team behind the proposal.

Local and niche directories

Agency directories and review platforms can bring leads and links. Choose a few credible ones, keep profiles complete and ask clients for reviews there as well as on Google.
