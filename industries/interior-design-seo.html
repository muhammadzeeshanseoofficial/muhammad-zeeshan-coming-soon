Overview

How interior design SEO showcases your portfolio

Interior design clients choose with their eyes, but Google needs words. Interior design SEO turns your portfolio into structured project pages with room, style, location and scope described in text, and adds service pages for full-service design, e-design, kitchen and bath design or commercial interiors.

Image search is a real traffic source for designers. Descriptive file names, alt text and fast-loading images help your work appear there. Pinterest and Houzz profiles support discovery too.

Many designers work across a wide area or remotely, so content that explains your process and fees helps qualify enquiries before the first call.

Turning a visual portfolio into searchable content

Interior designers often have beautiful websites with very little text, which makes it hard for Google to understand what each project was, where it was and what services it involved.

Adding a short written story to each project changes that: the brief, the constraints, the style, the key decisions and the result, with room names and the area mentioned naturally.

Organize projects so they support service pages

A kitchen project links to the kitchen design service, a commercial fit-out to commercial interiors. Use descriptive image file names and alt text so the work can appear in image search, and compress images so pages stay fast.

Finally, make the next step clear

Designers often lose enquiries because the contact page asks for too little or too much. A short form asking for project type, location, timing and budget range helps both sides.

Attracting the right clients, not just more enquiries

Interior designers rarely want every enquiry

A studio focused on full-home projects does not want dozens of requests for a single cushion consultation, and a designer focused on e-design does not want local site visits. Interior design SEO should attract the clients you actually want, and your pages can do much of that filtering.

Be clear about who you work with, typical project sizes, how you charge and where you work.

Show projects that represent the work you want more of, not everything you have ever done. A process page that explains each stage, from discovery to installation, helps the right clients feel confident and politely discourages poor fits.

Search Console data then shows which pages bring the enquiries that turn into projects, so you can invest in more of that content.
