Contractor SEO for remodels, additions and new builds

Contractor SEO helps general contractors and remodelers get found by homeowners planning a kitchen, bathroom, addition or full renovation, and gives them enough proof to request a quote.

Get a free SEO auditBook a call

What contractor SEO looks like in practice

Homeowners planning a remodel spend weeks researching. They search for ideas, costs, timelines and contractors near them, and they judge you on your portfolio long before they call. Contractor SEO is about matching that research journey: a page for each type of project you take on, galleries of completed work with location and scope, and honest guidance on cost ranges and timelines.

Local visibility still matters, because homeowners filter by area. Your profile, reviews and local SEO services work together with project pages to show you are established nearby. Licensing, insurance and warranties should be easy to find.

If you work mostly on large commercial projects, the construction SEO page covers the B2B side of the industry.

Why portfolios beat generic service pages for contractors

Most contractor websites list the same services in the same words, which gives Google little reason to rank one over another and gives homeowners little reason to call. Detailed project pages change that. A page about a real kitchen remodel in a named neighbourhood, with the layout problem, the materials chosen, the timeline and photos at each stage, is unique content no competitor can copy. It answers the questions homeowners actually have and naturally includes the terms they search for.

Link each project page to the matching service page and back again, so the service page gains supporting evidence and the project page gains context. Over a year, twenty good project pages can do more for rankings and conversions than any amount of generic copy.

Keep the details accurate and get the homeowner's permission before naming streets or showing the inside of their home.

Content ideas that attract general contractors customers

Helpful guides bring in people earlier in their research and support your service pages with internal links. These topics come from the questions customers of general contractors commonly ask:

  • How much does a kitchen remodel cost and what drives the price
  • Remodel timeline: design, permits, demolition, build and handover
  • Questions to ask a general contractor before signing
  • Design-build vs hiring an architect and contractor separately
  • How to live in your home during a renovation
  • Permits explained for common home projects in your area
  • Budgeting for an addition: what is included and what is extra
  • Before-and-after stories from completed local projects

What a high-converting contractor page includes

Ranking is only half the job; the page also has to turn a visitor into an enquiry. Every key page I build for this industry includes:

  • Project photos with scope and area for every service
  • A clear process from first call to final walkthrough
  • Licensing, insurance and warranty details
  • Cost guidance with honest ranges
  • Reviews from homeowners with similar projects
  • An easy consultation request with project type and budget fields

A 90-day contractor seo plan

  1. Map your profitable projects. List the project types you want more of and check which already have a dedicated page.
  2. Turn finished jobs into case studies. Photos, scope, timeline and challenges for each notable project, tagged by area.
  3. Build project pages. Kitchen, bath, additions and renovation pages with process, cost drivers and FAQs.
  4. Strengthen local trust. Licensing, insurance, warranties, reviews and directory listings kept consistent.
  5. Follow up on leads. Track which pages produce consultations and refine the content homeowners respond to.

Contractor seo across the US, UK, Canada, Australia and the UAE

In the UK homeowners search for builders and extensions rather than contractors and additions; in Australia they search for builders and renovators; in Canada and the US 'general contractor' is common. Using local vocabulary on each page helps both relevance and trust.

How results are measured

Count consultations and signed projects by source page. Remodeling leads have long cycles, so look at a three to six month window rather than week-to-week traffic.
