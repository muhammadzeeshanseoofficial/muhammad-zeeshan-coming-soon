Overview

What contractor SEO looks like in practice

Homeowners planning a remodel spend weeks researching. They search for ideas, costs, timelines and contractors near them, and they judge you on your portfolio long before they call. Contractor SEO is about matching that research journey: a page for each type of project you take on, galleries of completed work with location and scope, and honest guidance on cost ranges and timelines.

Local visibility still matters, because homeowners filter by area. Your profile, reviews and local SEO services work together with project pages to show you are established nearby. Licensing, insurance and warranties should be easy to find.

If you work mostly on large commercial projects, the construction SEO page covers the B2B side of the industry.

Why portfolios beat generic service pages for contractors

Most contractor websites list the same services in the same words, which gives Google little reason to rank one over another and gives homeowners little reason to call.

Detailed project pages change that.

A page about a real kitchen remodel in a named neighbourhood, with the layout problem, the materials chosen, the timeline and photos at each stage, is unique content no competitor can copy.

It answers the questions homeowners actually have and naturally includes the terms they search for.

Link each project page to the matching service page and back again, so the service page gains supporting evidence and the project page gains context.

Over a year, twenty good project pages can do more for rankings and conversions than any amount of generic copy.

Keep the details accurate and get the homeowner's permission before naming streets or showing the inside of their home.

Contractor seo across the US, UK, Canada, Australia and the UAE

In the UK homeowners search for builders and extensions rather than contractors and additions; in Australia they search for builders and renovators; in Canada and the US 'general contractor' is common.

Using local vocabulary on each page helps both relevance and trust.

How results are measured

Remodeling leads have long cycles, so look at a three to six month window rather than week-to-week traffic.
