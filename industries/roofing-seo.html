Overview

What roofing SEO covers for a roofing company

A roofing job is expensive and infrequent, so homeowners read more than they would for a plumber. They compare two or three roofers, look at photos of finished roofs, check reviews and try to work out a price range before they call. Roofing SEO is about being visible at each of those steps: in the map pack for roofer near me, on page one for roof replacement cost in your city, and in the reviews and photos people check last.

In practice that means separate pages for repair, replacement, storm damage and each roofing material you install, a Google Business Profile optimization that shows real jobs, and service-area pages only for the towns you actually cover. Roofing demand also follows the weather, so storm and hail content should be live before the season, not after it.

Unlike lead-generation platforms, the visibility you earn stays with your company. Most roofing clients start with a short audit of their site and profile, then a 90-day plan focused on the services with the best margins.

Roofing seo across the US, UK, Canada, Australia and the UAE

Roofing terms vary by country

US homeowners search for shingle roofs and hail damage, UK homeowners search for roofers, flat roofs and slipped tiles, Australian searches often mention roof restoration and Colorbond, and Canadian searches peak after ice-dam season. Pages should use the words your local customers use, and only cover the areas you actually serve.

How results are measured

Track calls and quote forms by page, not just rankings

In Search Console, watch impressions for repair, replacement and storm terms separately, and in your CRM tag each lead with its source so you know which roofing services organic search is actually paying for.
