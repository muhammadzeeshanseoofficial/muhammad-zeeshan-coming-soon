Painting SEO for interior, exterior and commercial painters

Painting SEO helps painting contractors appear when homeowners and property managers search for painters nearby, then shows enough finished work to earn the estimate request.

Get a free SEO auditBook a call

Where painting SEO makes the biggest difference

Painting is a visual trade, and searchers want to see results before they call.

Pages for interior painting, exterior painting, cabinet refinishing and commercial work should each have real before-and-after photos, a clear process and guidance on what affects price. That content also helps you rank for the specific job rather than a generic painters near me search alone.

Seasonality matters: exterior searches rise in spring and early summer in most of the US, UK and Canada.

Publishing and updating exterior content ahead of the season gives it time to rank. Your Google Business Profile optimization should show recent projects every month.

Commercial painting for property managers, offices and HOAs is a separate journey with different proof, such as insurance, crews and scheduling around tenants.

Using photos and reviews to win painting estimates

Painting buyers judge quality with their eyes, so photos do more selling than copy.

A consistent habit of photographing every job before, during and after, then uploading the best shots to your profile and the matching service page, builds a library of proof that competitors relying on stock images cannot match. Name the files descriptively and write alt text that says what the photo shows, such as an exterior repaint of a two-storey home.

Reviews work the same way

Ask at the final walkthrough when the customer is happiest, send a direct link, and reply to each review by mentioning the work done. Reviews that mention cabinets, exteriors or commercial projects help you appear for those specific searches and reassure the next customer.

Together, photos and reviews often move a painting company into the map pack faster than any other change.

Handling commercial painting enquiries

Commercial clients such as property managers, offices and retail units search for painting contractors who can work around tenants, schedule out of hours and provide insurance and safety documentation.

A dedicated commercial section with relevant projects, a capability statement and a simple bid request form helps you win these larger contracts.
