Overview

Why photography SEO is different from other local SEO

Photographers face a particular challenge: their best work is images, but search engines rank text. Many photography websites are beautiful galleries with almost no words, slow-loading images and pages named gallery-1. Photography SEO fixes that by giving each type of shoot its own page with written context, descriptive file names and alt text, fast images and clear booking information.

Searches are by type and place: wedding photographer [area], newborn photographer near me, corporate headshots [city], product photography. Each needs its own page that shows relevant work, explains the experience and gives pricing guidance.

Image search and AI-powered visual search bring real traffic to photographers, but only when images are optimized and pages load quickly.

Making image-heavy pages fast

Large photos slow pages, especially on phones, and slow pages rank and convert worse.

Export images at the size they are displayed, compress them, use modern formats such as WebP where possible, and lazy-load images below the fold. A gallery can still look stunning while loading quickly.

Check Core Web Vitals in Search Console regularly; photography sites are among the most likely to fail them.

Writing about shoots without losing the visual focus

Each gallery or story page needs a few short paragraphs: who the client was in general terms, where and when the shoot took place, the style and any interesting details.

Credit venues and suppliers where relevant. That text helps search engines understand the page and gives visitors context, without distracting from the images.

Over time, these stories become a searchable archive of your work

A couple searching for photos at a particular venue, or a business looking for headshots in a certain style, can land on a story that shows exactly what they want.

Niches attract better clients

Generalist photographers compete with everyone

Photographers known for a niche, such as elopements, newborns, food or architecture, rank more easily for that niche and command better prices. If you have a niche, make it obvious on your homepage and give it the most detailed page on your site.

Reviews and client experience

Clients choose photographers they feel comfortable with

Reviews that mention how you made people relax, how quickly galleries arrived and how easy you were to work with are powerful. Ask after gallery delivery and reply to every review.

Encourage clients to mention the type of shoot and location in their review, simply by asking what they enjoyed about their family session or wedding day.

Those natural details help your profile appear for those shoot types and give future clients a clearer picture.
