Overview

How tour company SEO wins bookings from OTAs

Travellers usually start with broad searches like things to do in Dubai or best food tours in Rome, then narrow to specific tours. Online travel agencies and review platforms dominate many of those results, which is why most operators depend on them and pay commission on every booking. Tour company SEO aims to win more of that journey directly.

That means tour pages that answer everything a traveller needs: itinerary, duration, meeting point, what is included, accessibility, cancellation policy, group size, photos and reviews. It also means destination and planning content that earns visibility earlier, and clear structured data so search engines and AI assistants understand your tours.

Your listings on OTAs and review platforms still matter. They build trust and send traffic. SEO adds a direct channel where you keep the margin and the customer relationship.

Getting cited by AI travel assistants

Travellers increasingly ask AI assistants for itineraries and tour recommendations

Those tools draw on pages that state facts clearly: what the tour is, where it starts, how long it takes, what it costs and what guests say about it. Tour pages written with that clarity, supported by structured data and consistent information across your site, Google profile and travel platforms, are more likely to be understood and referenced.

Original detail helps most: the guide's knowledge, the story behind the route, practical tips only a local operator would know.

Generic descriptions copied across platforms give AI systems and search engines no reason to prefer your page.

Tour company seo across the US, UK, Canada, Australia and the UAE

Tour searches depend on where travellers come from

Visitors from the US, UK, Canada and Australia search in English with different spellings and terms, while European visitors often search in their own languages. In the UAE, desert safaris and city tours dominate searches, while in Italy and France food, wine and history tours lead.

How results are measured

Track direct bookings and revenue from organic search, compare with OTA bookings and commission, and watch branded search growth as a sign of rising awareness.

Seasonality and planning windows for tours

Travellers plan at different times depending on the trip

Long-haul visitors often research weeks or months ahead, while city-break and local visitors search days before or even on the day. Publish and update seasonal tours, such as winter desert experiences in the UAE or summer food tours in Italy, well before the booking window opens, and keep availability and pricing accurate.
