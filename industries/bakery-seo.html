Overview

Two kinds of bakery searches, two kinds of pages

Bakery customers search in two different ways. Daily customers search for bakery near me, fresh bread or croissants open now and choose from the map pack. Celebration customers search for custom birthday cakes [city], wedding cakes or gluten-free cake days or weeks ahead, and they compare portfolios, prices and lead times.

Bakery SEO covers both. Your Google Business Profile optimization handles daily discovery with accurate hours, photos and attributes. Your website handles custom orders with gallery pages for each cake type, clear ordering steps, lead times, starting prices and an order enquiry form.

Wholesale is a third opportunity. Cafes, restaurants and offices search for bakery suppliers, and a wholesale page can bring steady accounts.

Bakery marketing also includes social media and local events, but organic search is often the channel that brings people with a specific order in mind.

How results are measured

Track custom cake enquiries and orders by page, wholesale enquiries and profile actions such as calls and directions.

During busy seasons, note how many custom orders came from people who found you on Google so you can plan capacity next year.

Cake galleries that rank in image search

Cake buyers often start in image search or social media, looking for ideas.

Gallery pages with clear categories, descriptive file names and alt text, such as a two-tier floral birthday cake, help your work appear there. Each gallery should link to the order page and mention sizes, flavours and starting prices.

Compress images so pages load quickly on phones, and avoid huge sliders that slow everything down.

Lead times and seasonal demand

Custom cake demand spikes around holidays, graduation season and wedding season

Publishing lead times clearly, and updating pages for seasonal orders such as holiday cakes and festive bakes before demand rises, helps you capture planned orders and set expectations.

Use profile posts to announce seasonal items and order deadlines, and keep holiday hours accurate.

Wholesale pages for cafes, restaurants and offices

Wholesale customers search differently from retail buyers

They want to know what you can supply regularly, minimum orders, delivery days, how ordering works and whether you can provide samples. A wholesale page that answers those points, with photos of your range and a simple enquiry form, can win steady accounts that smooth out daily sales.

Mention the types of businesses you already supply without naming them unless they agree, and link to the page from your footer and about page so it is easy to find.

Daily specials and opening hours

Daily customers want to know what is fresh and when you open.

Keep hours accurate, including early opening times and days you sell out early, and use profile posts to show the day's bakes. A simple page listing regular items by day, such as sourdough on certain mornings, helps regulars and gives Google more useful content about what you sell.
