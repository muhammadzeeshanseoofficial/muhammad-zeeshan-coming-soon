Coaching SEO for coaches who want more discovery calls

Coaching SEO helps business, executive, career and life coaches get found by the people they help best, and builds enough trust from content that visitors book a discovery call.

Get a free SEO auditBook a call

Why coaching SEO starts with a clear niche

Coaching is crowded and broad. A page that says you help people reach their potential competes with thousands of others and attracts few clients. Coaching SEO works when it is built around a clear niche and the specific problems that niche searches for: executive coach for new managers, career change coach, business coach for agency owners, leadership coaching for women in tech.

From there, offer pages explain each programme clearly: who it is for, the format, duration, what changes and how to start. Thought-leadership content shows how you think and earns visibility in both search and AI assistants that recommend coaches and frameworks.

Coaches who work locally benefit from a Google Business Profile optimization; most coaching is remote, so national or international visibility through content matters more.

Avoid income or results guarantees. Testimonials should be real, permission-based and typical rather than cherry-picked extremes.

Content ideas that attract business and career coaches customers

Helpful guides bring in people earlier in their research and support your service pages with internal links. These topics come from the questions customers of business and career coaches commonly ask:

  • Signs you need a coach
  • How coaching sessions work
  • Coaching vs mentoring vs therapy
  • Frameworks for your niche's core problem
  • Preparing for a discovery call
  • Leadership challenges for new managers
  • Career change planning steps
  • Measuring progress in coaching

What a high-converting coaching page includes

Ranking is only half the job; the page also has to turn a visitor into an enquiry. Every key page I build for this industry includes:

  • Who you help, stated clearly
  • Offer details and format
  • Testimonials
  • About and credentials
  • Pricing approach
  • Booking a call

A 90-day coaching seo plan

  1. Define niche and offers. Who you help and how.
  2. Offer pages. Each programme explained clearly.
  3. Pillar content. Deep guides on your niche's core problems.
  4. Proof. Testimonials and case stories with permission.
  5. Booking. Discovery call scheduling on every page.

Coaching seo across the US, UK, Canada, Australia and the UAE

UK and Australian clients search for business and career coaches; US clients for executive, leadership and life coaches; UAE clients for executive and leadership coaching in Dubai; Indian professionals for career and leadership coaching. Credentials and coaching bodies recognised also vary by market.

How results are measured

Track discovery calls booked by page, conversion to clients and which articles lead to bookings. Ask each new client which article or page convinced them to book.

Choosing a niche that search can support

A good coaching niche combines what you are best at, a group of people with a clear problem and enough search interest to build on. Keyword research shows how that group describes its problems, and the answers shape your pages. Narrower niches rank faster and convert better, and you can widen later.

Test a niche before rebuilding the whole site. Write one strong offer page and two or three articles for it, then watch Search Console impressions and discovery calls for a few months. If the niche gains traction, expand it; if not, adjust the angle rather than abandoning SEO.

Content that shows how you think

Coaching clients buy a person and a way of thinking. Long-form articles, frameworks, worksheets and honest case stories let prospects experience your approach before they call. Publish fewer, better pieces, each answering a real question your clients ask, and link every article to the relevant offer page.

This kind of content is also what AI assistants draw on when recommending approaches or coaches, so clarity and originality matter.

Building authority beyond your own site

Podcast appearances, guest articles, speaking at events and features in industry publications build your reputation and earn links. Choose outlets your niche actually reads. A media or speaking page listing appearances gives prospects more proof.

Credentials and trust

Mention recognised coaching qualifications, professional backgrounds and memberships of coaching bodies where relevant. Clear contracts, confidentiality and how sessions work reassure corporate buyers and individuals alike.

Corporate coaching buyers

If you coach inside organisations, HR and learning and development teams search for leadership programmes, team coaching and manager development. A corporate page with programme structures, how you measure progress and approved client examples helps you get shortlisted.
