Muhammad Zeeshan: background and recent work

I work as an independent SEO consultant and AI SEO specialist, focused on practical, measurable work: getting pages crawled and indexed, matching content to search intent, building authority the right way and making brands visible in AI-generated answers. My recent work includes ongoing SEO for FeetFinder, a large creator marketplace, and building SpicyRanked, my own review platform, from a young domain. Both are documented in the SEO case studies with Search Console and Ahrefs screenshots.

How I work

  • Direct contact: you talk to the person doing the work
  • Priorities based on impact and effort, explained in plain English
  • Work that follows Google's Search Essentials and spam policies
  • Rounded, dated figures in reports, never inflated claims
  • Clear ownership: your accounts, content and data stay yours

What I will not do

I do not buy links, build private networks, create fake reviews, publish mass-produced pages for every town, or promise first-page rankings. These tactics can work briefly but put the business at risk. I also do not take on health, medical, dental, legal, finance or insurance projects, so I can focus on sectors where I can add the most value.
