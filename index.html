My work covers the full range of search: technical SEO so pages can be crawled and indexed, on-page optimisation and helpful content that match what people search for, local SEO for businesses serving nearby customers, and AI search optimization so your brand can be cited by assistants as well as ranked in classic results.

How I Decide What to Work on First

Every Site Has More Possible Improvements Than Time or Budget Allows

I start with the issues that block visibility on pages that bring revenue, then the changes with the clearest upside, then everything else.

That order keeps early months productive and makes results easier to see.

For most businesses, the first wins come from fixing indexing problems, tightening the pages that already rank on page two, and making the Google Business Profile complete and accurate.
