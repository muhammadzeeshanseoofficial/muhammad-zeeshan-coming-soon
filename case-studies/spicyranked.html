Overview

About SpicyRanked

SpicyRanked is a review and ranking platform that compares creator and subscription websites for its readers. The site is my own project, and it is the clearest example of how I build organic growth on a young domain.

Website: spicyranked.com

The challenge

  • A young domain with limited authority competing against established review sites
  • A crowded niche where many competitors publish thin, near-identical reviews
  • Review and ranking pages that needed to be understood by both Google and AI answer engines
  • The need to grow traffic quickly without risky link building
SEO strategy

Technical, content, AEO/GEO and links

Technical SEO

  • Fast, crawlable templates for reviews and rankings
  • Clean internal linking between rankings, reviews and comparisons
  • Breadcrumbs, sitemaps and indexation monitoring
  • Structured data for reviews and FAQs

Content

  • Original review format with consistent criteria
  • Ranking and comparison pages built around search intent
  • Regular updates to keep rankings current
  • FAQ sections answering common reader questions

AEO and GEO

  • Direct-answer summaries at the top of reviews
  • Clear entities and facts AI systems can cite
  • Comparison tables and lists
  • Tracking visibility across AI platforms

Links and authority

  • Relevant outreach and mentions
  • No purchased links or private networks
  • Steady growth in referring domains
  • Brand consistency across profiles
Work done

What was delivered

Scope summary — owner to confirm

  • Planned the site architecture: rankings hubs, individual reviews and comparison pages
  • Built review templates with consistent scoring sections and FAQs
  • Published and refreshed content on a steady schedule
  • Monitored Search Console for indexation and query growth
  • Optimized pages for AI Overviews and AI assistants
  • Earned referring domains through outreach

Results

Results as of Nov 2026

As of Nov 2026, daily Google clicks grew roughly tenfold between late August and November 2026, with impressions rising in step. Over the same three months the site earned tens of thousands of clicks from millions of impressions at a top-10 average position. Ahrefs shows the site with a Domain Rating of 15, over 1,000 referring domains and around 1,800 organic keywords, plus citations across AI platforms.

Why this is an AI SEO case study

SpicyRanked was built from the start for both classic search and AI answers.

Reviews open with a short, direct summary, use consistent scoring criteria and include comparison tables and FAQs.

Entities such as the sites reviewed, their features and their pricing are described the same way across pages.

That structure helps Google understand the content and makes it easier for AI Overviews and assistants to quote it accurately.

Lessons from the project

A young domain can grow quickly when templates are clean and content matches intent

Consistent review formats help both readers and search engines

Refreshing ranking pages regularly keeps them competitive

Earned links and mentions build authority without the risks of paid links

Tracking AI visibility alongside Search Console shows the full picture

What would be different for a client site

Because SpicyRanked is my own project, decisions could be made quickly

Client projects involve approvals, development queues and brand guidelines, so timelines are usually longer. The principles, however, are the same, and they are described on the AI search optimization and SEO content services pages.
