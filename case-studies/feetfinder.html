Overview

About FeetFinder

FeetFinder is a large creator marketplace where creators sell digital content to buyers.

At this scale, SEO is less about starting growth and more about protecting and compounding it: keeping a big site technically healthy, defending top positions and staying visible as search shifts to AI answers.

Website: feetfinder.com

The Challenge

  • A large site with many page types that must stay crawlable and indexed
  • Defending top positions for brand and category searches against competitors
  • Keeping click-through rate high as AI Overviews change the results page
  • Maintaining a clean, high-authority backlink profile
SEO strategy

Technical, Content, AEO/GEO and Links

Technical SEO

  • Crawl and indexation monitoring across page types
  • Canonical, sitemap and internal link governance
  • Core Web Vitals and mobile performance
  • Structured data and FAQ markup

Content

  • Help and FAQ content answering creator and buyer questions
  • Titles and snippets written for high CTR
  • Content refreshes for slipping pages
  • Clear brand and pricing information

AEO and GEO

  • Consistent brand facts across the web
  • Answer-ready FAQ content
  • Tracking AI Overview and assistant citations
  • Entity and schema improvements

Authority

  • Ongoing link profile monitoring
  • Digital PR and relevant mentions
  • Unlinked mention reclamation
  • Competitor link gap reviews
Work done

What Was Delivered

Scope summary — owner to confirm

  • Ran technical audits and tracked fixes with the development team

  • Reviewed and verified FAQ and pricing content for accuracy

  • Monitored Search Console performance and indexation weekly

  • Analysed competitors and backlink profiles with Ahrefs

  • Improved titles and snippets on high-impression pages

  • Tracked visibility in AI answers

What FeetFinder SEO Involves at This Scale

At FeetFinder's size, SEO is about protecting and compounding what already works.

A large site with many page types can lose visibility quickly if a release breaks canonicals, a template change hides content or a sitemap fills with redirects.

The work therefore centres on monitoring, governance and steady improvement: catching technical issues early, keeping titles and snippets compelling, refreshing pages that slip and keeping brand information consistent for AI systems.

Lessons for Large Sites

  • Weekly Search Console monitoring catches indexing problems before they become traffic losses

  • Titles and snippets on high-impression pages are worth regular testing

  • Accurate FAQ and pricing content supports both users and AI answers

  • Link profile monitoring matters as much as new links

  • Working closely with developers keeps SEO part of every release
