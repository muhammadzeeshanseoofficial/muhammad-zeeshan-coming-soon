SEO services USA: what changes for American companies

The US search market is large and heavily contested. National brands, publishers, marketplaces and well-funded startups compete for the same commercial queries, and local markets in cities like New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Houston or Phoenix are crowded with service businesses that have invested in search for years. SEO services for American companies have to be precise: target the queries where you can realistically win, build pages that answer them better than anyone else and earn authority from sources US customers trust.

This page explains how I approach the US market. The full list of services is on the SEO services overview; here the focus is on what changes when the audience is American.

National versus local strategy

Some US businesses sell nationwide: online stores, SaaS products and professional services delivered remotely. Others serve a city, a metro area or a handful of states. National strategies focus on category pages, content clusters and digital PR. Local strategies focus on Google Business Profiles, location and service-area pages, reviews and local links. Many companies need a mix, such as a national brand with regional offices, and the plan reflects that.

State and city targeting without doorway pages

US businesses often want a page for every state or city. That works only when each page offers something real: an office or team there, local projects, state-specific rules or pricing, and reviews from local customers. Google's spam policies describe pages created just to rank for many locations as doorway pages. I build location pages where you have a genuine presence and use stronger service pages to cover the rest.

US directories, reviews and citations

For local businesses, the US citation landscape includes Google, Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, Yelp, the Better Business Bureau, Angi, Nextdoor and industry-specific directories such as Avvo or Healthgrades in their sectors. Consistent name, address and phone details across these matter, and reviews on Google and Yelp heavily influence choice.

Compliance and claims in US marketing

US advertising rules, including FTC guidance on endorsements and reviews, apply to marketing content. That means no fake reviews, clear disclosure of incentivised endorsements and honest claims. Regulated sectors such as healthcare, finance and legal have their own requirements. Content I create follows these principles.

AI search in the US market

American users adopted AI assistants early, and AI Overviews appear across many queries. Visibility in these features depends on the same foundations as traditional SEO plus consistent brand information and mentions in trusted US publications. The AI search optimization service covers this work.

Working across US time zones

I work remotely with US clients and schedule calls in Eastern, Central, Mountain or Pacific time. Day-to-day communication happens by email and WhatsApp, with a monthly report and review call. Invoices are in USD.

What to expect in the first 90 days

The first month covers an audit, keyword research for your US market and tracking. The second month focuses on the highest-impact technical and on-page fixes, plus the most important new pages. The third month adds content, local or national authority building and the first measurable movements in impressions and rankings. Competitive US markets usually need six to twelve months for significant growth.

How SEO services in the USA are priced

Pricing depends on site size, competition and how much work you want done for you. Typical options include a one-off audit, a project such as a migration or local SEO setup, and monthly retainers. The pricing page gives indicative ranges in USD, and the free website audit is a good way to start.

Measuring results for US clients

Reports focus on business outcomes: leads, calls, sales and pipeline from organic search, alongside visibility for priority queries. For multi-location businesses, results are broken down by location, and for national brands by product line or category, so you can see where growth comes from.

Why work with an independent consultant

An independent consultant gives you direct access to the person doing the work, without account managers in between. That keeps communication fast and decisions clear. For larger programmes, I work alongside your in-house team, developers and writers, or with specialist partners where needed.

Common SEO problems on US websites

The issues I see most on American sites are thin city pages created at scale, service pages that try to rank for everything at once, slow sites overloaded with tracking scripts and chat widgets, inconsistent business details across directories and content written for keywords rather than customers. Fixing these often produces gains before any new content is published.

SEO and paid search together

Many US companies spend heavily on Google Ads and Local Services Ads. SEO complements paid search by capturing the same customers organically over time. Paid search data also shows which queries convert best, which helps prioritise SEO work.
