Overview

Who This Privacy Policy Covers

Who I Am

This website, muhammad-zeeshan.com, is run by Muhammad Zeeshan, an independent SEO and AI SEO consultant. For the purposes of data protection law, I am the controller of the personal data described on this page.

What This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data I collect through this website, by email and on WhatsApp. It also explains why I collect it, who processes it for me and what rights you have.

Who I Work With

I work with clients in the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia and the United Arab Emirates. I also work with clients in France, Italy and India.

Data collected

The Personal Data I Collect

Contact and Free SEO Audit Forms

When you send the contact form or the free SEO audit form through WhatsApp, you give me your name, email address and website URL.

You may also add a message, your main market, your main goal or the service you are interested in.

Emails and WhatsApp Messages

If you email me or message me on WhatsApp, I receive your name, email address or phone number and whatever you choose to write. This can include details about your business and your website.

Analytics Data

This site uses the Google tag (GTM-M5JLBH6M) to run Google Analytics. It collects usage data such as pages viewed, approximate location, device, browser and how you arrived on the site, using cookies.

Hosting Server Logs

The site is hosted on Vercel, which records standard server logs when pages are requested. These logs can include your IP address, the page requested, the date and time, and your browser user agent.

What I Do Not Collect

I do not ask for payment card details on this website. I do not knowingly collect special category data, such as health information, through this site.

Purposes

How I Use Your Data

Replying to You

I use your contact details and message to reply to your enquiry or audit request. I also use them to prepare proposals and to deliver any work we agree.

Running the Website

I use analytics data to understand which pages are useful and to improve the site. Server logs help keep the site secure, available and free of errors.

Legal and Business Records

I may keep records of agreements, invoices and related correspondence where the law requires it. I may also keep them to establish or defend legal claims.

No Sale of Personal Data

I do not sell your personal data. I do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and I do not use it for automated decisions that have legal effects on you.

Legal bases

Legal Bases Under the GDPR and UK GDPR

Contract

Where you ask me for a proposal or we work together, I process your data to take steps before a contract and to perform it.

Legitimate Interests

I rely on legitimate interests to reply to general enquiries, keep the site secure and improve it. You can object to this at any time.

Consent

Where the law requires consent, such as for non-essential cookies in the UK and the EU, I rely on your consent. You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing.

Legal Obligation

I keep some records, such as invoices, because tax and accounting laws require it.

Sharing

Who Processes Your Data

Service Providers

I use a small number of service providers to run this site and my business. They process data on my behalf and under their own privacy terms.

  • Google

    Google Analytics, the Google tag and Gmail for email. Google describes its own handling of data in the Google Privacy Policy.

  • Vercel

    Website hosting and server logs.

  • WhatsApp

    Messages you send me on WhatsApp.

  • Website forms

    The contact and free SEO audit forms do not store your details on this website. Sending a form opens WhatsApp with the details filled in, and the message is delivered through WhatsApp once you press send.

Other Disclosures

I only disclose personal data to others if the law requires it, or to protect my legal rights. I do not share your enquiry with other clients.

Transfers

International Data Transfers

Where Your Data Goes

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own. This includes the countries where I work and where Google, Vercel and WhatsApp operate their servers, such as the United States.

Safeguards

Where UK or EU data protection law applies, transfers rely on safeguards such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses offered by these providers. You can ask me for more detail about these safeguards.

Retention

How Long I Keep Data

Enquiries

I keep enquiries and audit requests that do not lead to a project only for as long as needed to reply and follow up. After that, I delete them.

Client Records

For clients, I keep project correspondence for the length of the work and a reasonable period afterwards. I keep invoices and agreements for as long as tax and accounting rules require.

Analytics and Logs

Google Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in the Google Analytics property. Vercel keeps server logs for a limited period under its own retention settings.

Your rights

Your Privacy Rights

UK, EU and EEA (GDPR and UK GDPR)

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable format.

You can also withdraw consent and complain to your data protection authority, such as the ICO in the UK.

California (CCPA and CPRA)

If these laws apply, you can ask to know what personal information I hold, and to have it corrected or deleted.

You can also opt out of sale or sharing, and you will not be treated differently for using your rights.

California Categories

The categories I collect are identifiers such as name, email and phone number, and internet activity such as analytics usage data. I collect them from you directly and from your device, for the purposes listed above.

Canada (PIPEDA)

You can ask to access and correct your personal information and withdraw consent, subject to legal limits. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Australia (Privacy Act 1988)

Under the Australian Privacy Principles, you can ask to access and correct your personal information. If you are not satisfied with my response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Other Countries

If you live elsewhere, including the UAE, France, Italy or India, you have the rights your local data protection law gives you. I will handle your request in line with that law.

Security, Children and Changes
