Plumber SEO

Plumbing SEO that brings plumbers more emergency and planned calls

Plumbing SEO helps plumbers and plumbing companies appear first when local customers search for urgent repairs and planned jobs, and turns those searches into calls and booked work.

Get a free SEO auditBook a call

How customers search for a plumber

Most plumbing searches are urgent: emergency plumber near me, burst pipe, blocked drain, no hot water. People call quickly, usually from a phone, and choose from the first few results based on availability, reviews and distance. Planned searches such as bathroom installation, water heater replacement cost or repipe a house involve more research. Plumbing SEO targets both.

Plumbing SEO pages that win calls

  • Emergency plumbing page with clear availability, response times and a large call button
  • Separate pages for drains, leaks, water heaters or boilers, toilets, bathrooms and gas work where licensed
  • Commercial plumbing page if you serve businesses
  • Service-area pages for towns you genuinely cover
  • Pricing guidance, call-out fees or how quotes work
  • Licences, insurance, guarantees and reviews

Each page should make it obvious within seconds what you do, where you work and how to call.

Ranking in the map pack

For emergency searches, the map pack often matters more than organic results. A complete profile with the right primary category, accurate service areas, real photos of vans and work, steady reviews and correct hours (including whether you offer 24-hour service) is essential. Service-area plumbers should hide their home address. More detail is on the Google Business Profile optimization page.

Service-area pages done properly

Many plumbers serve dozens of towns. Copying the same page with a different town name is a doorway pattern and rarely works. Strong area pages mention jobs completed there, local issues such as hard water or older pipework, response times and reviews from customers in the area. Start with the towns that bring the most profitable work.

Content that builds trust before the call

Guides on what to do when a pipe bursts, how to find the stopcock, signs a water heater is failing or what affects bathroom installation costs help customers in stressful moments and show expertise. Safety advice should be accurate and reviewed by a qualified plumber.

Reviews: the deciding factor

When several plumbers appear together, reviews often decide who gets the call. Ask every satisfied customer, send a direct link by text after the job and reply to every review. Recent reviews matter, so a steady routine beats occasional bursts.

Lead generation sites versus your own SEO

Lead platforms can fill gaps, but you pay for each lead and compete with other plumbers on the same enquiry. Your own rankings bring direct calls that belong to you. Many plumbers use both while building their own visibility.

Tracking calls and jobs

Call tracking on the website and profile, a simple way to log the source of each job and monthly reports on calls, booked jobs and revenue by source show what SEO brings in, rather than relying on rankings alone.

Plumbing SEO in different countries

Terms vary: plumber and water heater in the US, boiler repair, Gas Safe engineer and blocked drains in the UK, hot water system in Australia, plumbing services Dubai in the UAE. Directories also differ, such as Checkatrade in the UK, hipages in Australia and HomeStars in Canada.

A typical first 90 days

Month one covers the audit, profile fixes, call tracking and the emergency page. Month two adds core service pages and a review routine. Month three adds priority area pages, helpful guides and local links from suppliers, associations and community sponsorships. Each month's report shows calls and jobs compared with the previous period.

Website speed and mobile usability

Most plumbing searches happen on phones, often in stressful moments. A slow page, a hidden phone number or a pop-up covering the screen loses the call to a competitor. The website should load quickly on mobile data, show the phone number and a call button at the top of every page and keep forms short. These basics often improve calls from existing traffic before rankings change.

Common plumbing SEO mistakes

Buying cheap backlink packages, adding dozens of copied town pages, using stock photos of other people's vans, leaving old phone numbers on directories and ignoring the profile are the problems I see most. Each one weakens trust with customers and search engines.

Fixing them is usually the first step.
