Local SEO

Local SEO services that bring more calls, visits and map pack rankings

Local SEO services help businesses appear when nearby customers search for what they offer, through accurate profiles, location pages, consistent citations, reviews and local authority.

Get a free SEO auditBook a call

How local SEO services work

Local rankings depend on three broad factors that Google describes publicly: relevance, distance and prominence.

You cannot change where a searcher is standing, but you can make your business clearly relevant for the services you offer and more prominent through reviews, links, mentions and a strong website. Local SEO services organise that work into a repeatable system.

The Google Business Profile optimization service covers the profile itself in depth.

This page covers everything around it: your website's location and service pages, citations, reviews process, local links and reporting.

Location pages that are not doorway pages

Google's spam policies warn against doorway pages: many near-identical pages created only to rank for each city.

Useful location pages are different.

They include real information about serving that area, such as the team or branch, service coverage, local projects with photos, reviews from customers in that area, opening hours, travel or parking details and answers to local questions.

I build fewer, better location pages rather than hundreds of templated ones.

For service-area businesses without a storefront, service-area pages follow the same principle: include them only where you genuinely work, and make each one useful.

Citations and consistent business data

Your business name, address, phone number and hours appear on many directories, maps and data aggregators.

Inconsistent or duplicate listings confuse customers and dilute trust. The citation work starts with an audit of existing listings, then fixes errors, removes duplicates and adds the core directories that matter in your country, such as Apple Business Connect and Bing Places everywhere, Yelp in the US, Yell in the UK, YellowPages in Canada, True Local in Australia and established UAE directories.

Reviews: a process, not a campaign

Reviews influence both rankings and whether people call

The most reliable approach is a simple routine: ask every happy customer at the right moment, make leaving a review easy with a direct link, and reply to every review, positive or negative. I never use fake reviews, review gating or incentives that break platform rules.

Local links and mentions

Local prominence grows with genuine mentions: local news coverage, sponsorships, community events, supplier and partner pages, chambers of commerce and trade associations.

These links are often modest in authority but highly relevant, and they are hard for competitors to copy.

Local SEO reporting that tracks real leads

Rankings in a single location tell you little, because local results change street by street.

I use grid-based rank tracking across your service area alongside the numbers that matter: calls, direction requests, form leads and bookings from search. Reports show what changed, why and what comes next.

Local SEO pairs naturally with industry-specific work, such as HVAC SEO, plumbing SEO and cleaning company SEO.

Local SEO for different countries

Local search works similarly across countries, but directories, review habits and search terms differ.

US businesses rely heavily on Yelp and industry directories; UK customers often check Yell, Checkatrade or Trustpilot; Australian customers use True Local and hipages; Canadian customers use YellowPages.ca and Bing more than many expect; and in the UAE, bilingual Arabic and English listings and WhatsApp contact matter. The local SEO plan adapts to the country you serve.

Common local SEO mistakes to avoid

Creating profiles at virtual offices or addresses where you do not serve customers, stuffing city names into the business name, publishing dozens of identical city pages, buying reviews and letting old phone numbers linger on directories are all common, and each can cost rankings or trigger suspensions.

Most of the early work in a local SEO engagement is undoing these issues and setting up a clean, honest foundation that keeps working.

  • Calls from search are flat or falling
