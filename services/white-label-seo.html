White Label SEO

White label SEO services delivered quietly under your agency's brand

White label SEO lets marketing, web design and digital agencies offer expert SEO to their clients without hiring a full team, with the work delivered under the agency's own brand.

Get a free SEO auditBook a call

How white label SEO works

You sell SEO to your clients and manage the relationship. I deliver the work behind the scenes: audits, technical fixes, on-page optimisation, content and reporting, all in your branding. Clients deal with you; my name does not appear unless you choose to introduce me as part of your team.

What I can deliver for your agency

  • Full SEO audits in your report template
  • Technical SEO fixes and migration support
  • On-page optimisation and keyword mapping
  • Content briefs and articles
  • Local SEO and Google Business Profile work
  • Monthly reports with your logo and commentary
  • Pre-sales audits to help you win clients

Confidentiality and non-solicitation

Trust is the foundation of white label work. I sign a non-disclosure and non-solicitation agreement, never contact your clients directly unless you ask me to, and never approach them afterwards. Files, access and reports are handled through your systems where possible.

Processes that fit your agency

Every agency works differently. We agree how briefs arrive, how access is shared, turnaround times, how questions are handled and what reports look like. A shared task board or your project management tool keeps everything visible to your account managers.

Quality and ethics

Work delivered under your brand reflects on you, so it follows Google's guidelines: no paid link schemes, no doorway pages and no automated low-quality content. If a client requests tactics that would put their site at risk, I explain the risks so you can advise them.

Pre-sales support

Winning a client often depends on showing expertise early. I can prepare a short audit or opportunity analysis for prospects under your brand, helping your sales team have specific, credible conversations.

Pricing models

White label work is usually priced per project for audits and fixes, or as a monthly fee per client for ongoing SEO. You set your own client pricing. The pricing page explains how engagements are structured.

Agencies this suits

Web design studios, social media and PPC agencies, branding agencies and consultants who want to add SEO without building a team. If you are looking to grow your own agency's leads through search, see marketing agency SEO instead, which is about ranking your agency, not serving your clients.

Getting started

We start with one client as a pilot, agree the process and refine it before adding more. Email muhammadzeeshanhaideroffical@gmail.com or message +92 301 9343535 on WhatsApp to discuss your agency's needs.

Communication and turnaround

Clear communication keeps white label work smooth. Each client has a named point of contact on your side, a shared task list and agreed response times. Monthly work is scheduled at the start of the month, reports arrive before your client meetings, and urgent issues, such as a traffic drop or a site outage, are flagged the same day so your team can inform the client first.

Reporting your clients will understand

Reports focus on the outcomes your clients care about: leads, sales, visibility for important services and progress on the agreed plan. Technical detail is available when needed, but the summary is written in plain language, ready for your account managers to present. You can edit and add your own commentary before sending.

Scaling SEO in your agency

As your SEO client base grows, processes matter more. I help create audit templates, onboarding checklists, monthly task lists and reporting standards that make delivery consistent across clients, whether the work is done by me, your team or both.

Onboarding a new client

Each new client starts with a short intake: goals, services, locations, competitors, access and past SEO work. A baseline audit follows, then a plan for the first three months that you approve before presenting it. This makes delivery predictable and gives your client a clear picture from the start.

Tools and access

I work with standard tools for crawling, rank tracking and reporting, and can use your agency's accounts so data stays with you. Client access to Search Console, analytics and the CMS is requested through your agency, and removed when work ends.

All tool costs are agreed upfront, with no hidden charges added to your client invoices.
