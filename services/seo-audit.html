SEO audit services with a prioritised action plan, not a raw export

SEO audit services give you a full, expert review of your website's technical health, content, search intent, internal links, authority and competitors, with a clear plan of what to fix first.

What a full SEO audit covers

  • Crawlability, indexing, canonicals, redirects and sitemaps
  • Page speed, Core Web Vitals and mobile usability
  • Search intent match for your most important pages
  • Keyword mapping and cannibalisation
  • Content quality, depth and E-E-A-T signals

Each finding includes the evidence, why it matters, the recommended fix and an estimate of impact and effort.

Paid SEO audit services vs the free audit

The free website audit is a quick review that highlights a few of the most visible issues on your site, so you can decide whether deeper work is worthwhile. The paid audit described here is a full investigation: every important template, your Search Console data, competitors and a prioritised roadmap. The two are designed for different stages, and the free review never tries to replace the full audit.

How I prioritise audit findings

An audit is only useful if it changes what your team does next. I group findings into three bands: fix now (issues blocking indexing or revenue pages), fix next (improvements with clear upside) and monitor (minor issues). The first band is usually short, so work can start immediately.

Who SEO audit services are for

Businesses that have lost traffic and do not know why, sites planning a redesign or migration, teams taking over an existing site, and companies that want an independent view before hiring an agency or committing to a retainer. Agencies also use the audit to review client sites under a white label arrangement.

After the audit

You can implement the plan with your own team, ask me to implement it, or combine both. A follow-up review after the main fixes confirms what changed in crawling, indexing and performance. Contact details for booking are on the contact page, or email muhammadzeeshanhaideroffical@gmail.com.

What you receive at the end of the audit

The audit is delivered as a written report and a working spreadsheet. The report explains the overall picture in plain language: what is holding the site back, what is working and where the biggest opportunities are. The spreadsheet lists every finding with affected URLs, priority, owner and status, so your team can track progress.

I also walk through the findings on a call, answer questions from developers and content writers, and agree the first set of fixes. For most sites, the first two weeks after the audit are the most productive, because the highest-impact issues are clear and usually quick to fix.

How long a website SEO audit takes

A small business site is typically reviewed within a week. A larger site, an online store with thousands of products or a multi-country site with hreflang needs two to three weeks, because templates, indexing patterns and international setup each need careful testing. Timelines are agreed upfront and depend on access to Search Console and analytics.

Audit methods and tools

I combine a full crawl of the site, Search Console performance and indexing reports, analytics data, manual review of key templates and a look at the search results themselves. Tools speed up the data collection, but every important finding is checked by hand, because automated scores often flag harmless issues and miss the ones that matter, such as a page targeting the wrong intent or two pages competing for the same query.

Where a finding depends on Google's guidance, such as canonicalisation, structured data eligibility or spam policies, the recommendation follows the current documentation rather than rules of thumb.

Signs you need a website SEO audit

Organic traffic falling without an obvious reason, important pages missing from Google, a redesign on the horizon, rankings stuck despite publishing regularly, or a new team inheriting a site with an unknown history are the most common triggers. An audit turns those uncertainties into a clear list of causes and fixes.

A note on audit scores

Many tools produce a single health score. It can be a useful snapshot, but it is not a Google metric and it treats very different issues as equal. I include a simple score for tracking progress, explained clearly, and always prioritise the findings by their real impact on crawling, indexing, relevance and conversions rather than by how they affect the score.
