Real Estate SEO

Real estate SEO that brings buyer, seller and landlord leads

Real estate SEO helps agents, brokerages and property developers rank for local property searches, attract sellers and landlords as well as buyers, and compete where the big portals leave gaps.

Get a free SEO auditBook a call

Where agents can compete with portals

Portals dominate broad searches like homes for sale in [city]

Agents win in places portals do not cover well: neighbourhood expertise, seller searches such as sell my house in [area] or property valuation [area], agent and agency name searches, new developments, niche property types and local questions. Real estate SEO focuses effort where an independent agency can realistically rank.

Neighbourhood pages with real knowledge

Buyers and renters want to know what living in an area is like: schools, transport, prices, property types, amenities, character and recent sales.

Pages written by agents who know the area, with original photos and honest pros and cons, rank for neighbourhood searches and show expertise. Template pages with swapped names do not.

Seller and landlord pages

Sellers and landlords are often the most valuable leads

Pages explaining your selling process, fees, marketing approach, valuations and results, along with landlord services such as letting and property management, rank for high-intent searches and convert well.

Listings and technical SEO

Listing pages appear and disappear constantly, which creates technical challenges: thin duplicate content from feeds, expired listings returning errors and crawl waste from search filters.

Good practice includes unique descriptions, clear handling of sold or let properties, controlled indexing of filter combinations and fast pages with optimised images.

Agent profiles and E-E-A-T

Property decisions involve large sums, so trust matters

Agent profiles with experience, areas covered, licences or registrations, reviews and sales history help both clients and search engines understand who is behind the advice. Content touching on mortgages, law or tax should be reviewed by qualified professionals or point readers to them.

Google Business Profile for agencies

Each office should have an accurate profile with the right category, photos of the team and office, reviews from buyers, sellers and landlords and regular posts about market updates.

Individual agents can have practitioner profiles where guidelines allow.

Developers and new projects

Property developers can rank for new project names, off-plan searches and area-plus-project-type queries.

Project pages need floor plans, specifications, location details, payment plans where relevant, progress updates and clear enquiry routes. This is especially common in the UAE, where off-plan searches are significant.

Market reports and local authority

Regular local market updates, based on public data and your own experience, attract links from local media and give clients reasons to return.

Clear sourcing and dates keep them trustworthy.

Real estate SEO across markets

Terms differ: realtor and homes for sale in the US and Canada, estate agent and letting agent in the UK, real estate agent in Australia, property for sale Dubai and off-plan in the UAE.

Regulations on advertising and licensing differ too, and pages must follow local rules.

Measuring real estate SEO

I track valuation requests, viewing enquiries, landlord leads and calls from organic search, as well as rankings for neighbourhood and seller searches.

Lead quality matters more than volume, so the source of each instruction is recorded where possible.

Valuation tools and lead capture

Online valuation tools and forms attract sellers early, but they need to be useful and honest.

A simple estimate with a clear explanation of its limits, followed by an offer of a proper valuation from a local agent, builds trust. The page around the tool should explain how valuations work and what affects price.

Images, virtual tours and speed

Property pages rely on large photos, floor plans and tours, which can slow pages heavily.

Correctly sized, compressed images, lazy loading for galleries and lightweight embeds keep pages fast for buyers browsing on phones.

Rental and property management searches

Tenants search for rentals by area and property type, and landlords search for letting agents and management services.

Separate pages for rentals and landlord services ensure each audience finds the right information and that the pages do not compete with sales content.

Commercial property

Agencies dealing with offices, retail units, warehouses or land need separate commercial pages for each property type and service, such as sales, leasing and investment.

Business buyers search differently from home buyers, with terms like square footage, lease terms and use class, and expect detailed specifications.

Investment pages should explain yields and risks carefully, without promising returns
