Destination pages with real expertise

Travellers want honest, specific advice: when to go, how long to stay, where to base yourself, how to get around and what it costs.

Destination pages written by people who have been there, with original photos, sample itineraries and practical tips, outperform copied descriptions and show the experience Google's guidance values.

Trip type and itinerary pages

Searches such as family safari Kenya, honeymoon in the Maldives, 10 day Italy itinerary or small group tours Japan combine destination and trip type.

Pages for these combinations, linked from destination pages, attract travellers who are close to booking.

Seasonal timing

Travel research often starts months before the trip

Pages for summer holidays need to rank in winter and spring; ski trips need visibility in late summer. Publishing and updating content well ahead of booking season is essential.

Technical issues on travel sites

Booking engines, search filters and supplier feeds can create duplicate pages, slow load times and thin content.

I check crawlability, indexing of filter pages, speed with heavy images and correct handling of expired offers and departures.

Travel SEO and trust

Travellers spend large amounts in advance, so trust is essential

Show licences and protection schemes where they apply, such as ATOL or ABTA in the UK, company details, reviews, team expertise and clear booking terms.

Content that inspires and converts

Guides, packing lists, visa and entry overviews (with links to official sources described rather than copied), budget breakdowns and seasonal guides attract travellers at the dreaming and planning stages, then link to itineraries and enquiry forms.

Hotels, tours and transfers

Hotels, tour operators and transfer companies have their own search patterns, covered on the hotel SEO, tour company SEO and airport transfer SEO pages.

This page focuses on travel agencies and holiday companies selling trips.

International travel audiences

A UK travel company may also sell to Irish customers; a UAE agency may serve residents from many countries.

Hreflang, currency, departure airports and local terms (holiday versus vacation) need careful handling, explained on the international SEO page.

Measuring travel SEO

I track enquiries, bookings and revenue by destination and trip type, alongside rankings and seasonal visibility, so investment follows the destinations that sell.

A typical engagement

It begins with an audit and keyword research by destination and trip type, then prioritises the destinations with the most commercial value and the strongest expertise.

Content and technical work follow, scheduled around booking seasons.

Common travel SEO mistakes

Publishing hundreds of thin destination pages from supplier feeds, using stock photos only, letting departure dates and prices go out of date, hiding company details and relying entirely on a booking engine that search engines cannot crawl are frequent problems.

Each reduces trust and visibility.

Travel content and AI search

Travellers increasingly ask assistants for itinerary ideas and recommendations

Clear, expert destination content, consistent company information and mentions in respected travel publications improve the chances of being cited. The AI search optimization page explains this further.

Links and partnerships for travel brands

Tourism boards, local partners, travel writers, podcasts and niche communities provide relevant links and mentions.

Original content such as data on traveller trends or expert guides to lesser-known destinations gives them a reason to reference you.

Group, corporate and event travel

Group trips, school travel, corporate retreats and event travel are often overlooked by large booking sites.

Pages explaining how you organise group travel, the destinations you know well, what is included and how quotes work can rank for specific, high-value searches.

Keeping content accurate

Travel information changes: entry rules, prices, opening times and transport options

Each important page should show when it was last reviewed, and a schedule of updates keeps the site trustworthy and avoids misleading travellers.

Travel SEO for small agencies

Small agencies often have deep knowledge of a few destinations or trip styles.

Focusing content on those strengths, rather than trying to cover the whole world, builds authority faster and attracts travellers who value specialist advice. A small number of excellent destination and itinerary pages, kept up to date and linked well, can outperform much larger sites in that niche.

Expand only once those pages perform
