Google Business Profile

Google Business Profile optimization for more calls and map visibility

Google Business Profile optimization makes your profile complete, accurate and active, so it appears for more local searches in Google Maps and turns more views into calls, visits and bookings.

Get a free SEO auditBook a call

What Google Business Profile optimization covers

Choosing the most accurate primary and secondary categories

Services, products and attributes filled in fully

Business description written for customers, without keyword stuffing

Correct hours, special hours, service areas and contact options

Photos and videos of the real business, team and work

Regular posts for offers, events and updates

A review request and response process

Questions and answers kept accurate

Links to the right landing pages, with tracking

Following Google's profile guidelines

Google publishes guidelines for representing your business, and breaking them can lead to suspension.

The business name must match real-world branding, without added keywords or locations.

Profiles need a real address where customers are served, or a hidden address for service-area businesses.

Virtual offices and co-working addresses are generally not eligible unless staffed during business hours. I follow these rules strictly, because a suspended profile loses all its visibility.

Categories: the most important choice

The primary category strongly influences which searches a profile appears for

I review the categories used by top competitors, your actual services and Google's category list, then choose a primary category that matches your main service and a few accurate secondary categories.

Photos, posts and activity

Real photos of premises, team, vehicles and finished work help customers decide and show that the business is active.

Posts are useful for offers, seasonal services and events. Neither is a magic ranking lever, but a complete, current profile converts more of the people who see it.

Reviews and replies

Review count, rating and recency influence both local visibility and customer choice.

I set up a simple way for your team to request reviews after each job, provide reply templates you can personalise and flag reviews that break Google's policies for removal requests. Fake reviews and paid reviews are never used.

Connecting the profile with your website

The website link on your profile should point to the most relevant page, usually the homepage for single-location businesses or a location page for multi-location brands.

That page should repeat the same name, address and phone details and explain your services clearly. This connection is part of the wider local SEO services, which also cover citations and location pages.

Handling suspensions, duplicates and edits

Profiles can be suspended, duplicated or edited by others

I help resolve duplicate listings, respond to suggested edits, prepare evidence for reinstatement requests and set up regular checks so problems are spotted quickly.

Who benefits most

Service-area businesses such as plumbers and HVAC companies, restaurants, salons, gyms and any business where customers search with near me or a city name.

Products, services and booking links

The services and products sections let you show exactly what you offer, often with descriptions and prices.

Many businesses leave them empty.

Filling them in helps customers understand the offer and gives Google more context about relevance.

Where supported, booking, ordering or appointment links reduce friction for customers ready to act, and messaging options can be switched on if your team can reply quickly.

Monthly profile care

Profiles benefit from steady activity: new photos after jobs, posts for seasonal offers, updated special hours before holidays, replies to every review and checks for unwanted edits.

I provide a simple monthly routine your team can follow, or handle it for you, with a short report showing views, calls, website clicks and direction requests compared with the previous period.

Google Business Profile optimization in different countries

The profile works the same way worldwide, but customer habits differ

In the UAE, profiles often need English and Arabic details and WhatsApp-friendly contact options; in the UK and Australia, service-area businesses are especially common; in Canada, bilingual French and English details may help in Quebec. The setup follows each market's needs while keeping within Google's rules.

Mistakes that hold profiles back

Choosing a broad category instead of the most specific one, leaving hours out of date, uploading only stock photos, ignoring reviews, letting duplicate listings exist and linking to a slow or unrelated web page are the most common problems.

Fixing them is usually the quickest local win available, often within weeks.
