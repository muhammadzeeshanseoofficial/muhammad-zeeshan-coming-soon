Small Business SEO

Small business SEO that brings customers without a big budget

Small business SEO focuses limited time and budget on the few things that bring customers fastest: a strong Google profile, clear service pages, local visibility and reviews.

Get a free SEO auditBook a call

What small business SEO should focus on

Small businesses do not need the same programme as a national brand.

Most of the results come from a short list: a complete and active Google Business Profile, a fast mobile website with a clear page for each main service, consistent business details across directories, a steady flow of reviews and a few helpful pages that answer common customer questions. Small business SEO prioritises that list and skips work that will not pay back.

A practical first 90 days

Weeks 1–2: audit the website, profile and local listings; fix urgent issues

Weeks 3–6: improve or create service pages; set up tracking for calls and forms

Weeks 7–10: clean citations; start a review routine

Weeks 11–13: publish helpful answers to common questions; review results

By the end, the foundations are in place and you know which activities to continue.

Doing some of the work yourself

Many owners want to stay involved to save money

I can set up the foundations and then give you a simple monthly routine: photos and posts on the profile, review requests, small content updates. You handle what you are comfortable with, and I handle the technical and strategic parts.

Avoiding common small business SEO traps

Cheap packages promising page-one rankings, hundreds of directory submissions, bulk backlinks or auto-generated city pages can harm a small site.

So can redesigns that change URLs without redirects. I explain the risks clearly, and nothing is done to your site that you do not understand.

Websites built on simple platforms

Wix, Squarespace, WordPress, GoDaddy and Shopify all support good SEO when set up properly.

I work within the platform you have rather than insisting on a rebuild, unless the site truly cannot be fixed.

Local and online businesses

Most small businesses serve local customers, so local SEO and Google Business Profile optimization are central.

Small online shops focus more on product and category pages, covered by ecommerce SEO.

Transparent pricing and no long contracts

Small business budgets need predictability

Engagements are monthly with clear deliverables, and you can stop with notice. The pricing page explains typical costs, and the free website audit is a no-obligation way to see what your site needs first.

Measuring what matters to owners

Rankings are interesting, but owners care about calls, bookings, enquiries and sales.

I set up simple tracking for those and report in plain language each month: what was done, what changed and what is next.

Industries I work with

Trades, home services, restaurants, salons, gyms, professional services, retailers and many more.

Industry-specific pages, such as plumbing SEO or restaurant SEO, explain the details for each sector.

Small business SEO by country

The basics are the same everywhere, but directories and habits differ

US small businesses benefit from Yelp, the Better Business Bureau and industry directories; UK businesses from Yell, Thomson Local and trade platforms; Canadian businesses from YellowPages.ca; Australian businesses from True Local and Hotfrog; UAE businesses from bilingual listings and WhatsApp-friendly contact options. The plan adapts to where your customers are.

Reviews as a small business advantage

Small businesses often know their customers personally, which makes asking for reviews easier than for larger competitors.

A short message after each job with a direct review link, followed by a friendly reply to each review, builds a steady stream that helps both rankings and trust.

Service pages that win customers

Each main service deserves its own page explaining what is included, who it suits, the areas covered, typical prices or how quotes work, photos of real work, reviews and an easy way to call or book.

One generic page listing everything rarely ranks for specific searches, and it gives visitors little reason to choose you.

Keeping SEO simple and sustainable

The best small business SEO is the kind you can keep doing.

After setup, a monthly routine of around an hour covering reviews, profile updates, a new photo set and one content improvement keeps momentum going without overwhelming a busy owner.
