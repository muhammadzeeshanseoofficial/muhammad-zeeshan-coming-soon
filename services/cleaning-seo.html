Overview

How people search for cleaning services

Residential customers search for house cleaning near me, deep cleaning, end of tenancy cleaning or move out cleaning. Businesses search for office cleaning [city], commercial cleaning company or janitorial services. The two groups want different things: households want trust, price and availability; businesses want reliability, insurance, staff vetting and contracts. Cleaning services SEO builds separate paths for each.

Specialist cleaning has its own pages

Carpet, window and exterior cleaning attract different searches and often different customers.

If you specialise in them, the dedicated pages for carpet cleaning SEO, window cleaning SEO and pressure washing SEO cover those in depth. This page focuses on general residential and commercial cleaning companies.

Google Business Profile for cleaners

Most cleaning companies are service-area businesses, so the address should be hidden and service areas listed accurately.

Choose the most specific category, add every service, upload real photos of your team and results, and keep a steady flow of reviews. Before-and-after photos are especially persuasive.

Trust signals that win bookings

Letting someone into a home or office requires trust

Show insurance, background checks, training, consistent cleaners where you offer them, satisfaction guarantees, eco-friendly products if used and genuine reviews. Clear policies on cancellations, keys and breakages answer common worries.

Online booking and instant quotes

Many customers prefer to book online

A simple quote calculator or booking form, with clear prices for common jobs, increases conversions and helps pages rank for price-related searches. Keep forms short and mobile-friendly.

Commercial cleaning SEO

Commercial buyers search less often but sign long contracts

Pages for offices, retail, healthcare, schools and industrial sites, with case studies, compliance information and a tender-friendly contact route, attract facility managers. LinkedIn and local business networks support these pages with mentions.

Content that answers cleaning questions

Guides such as what a deep clean includes, how often offices should be cleaned, end of tenancy checklists or how to prepare for a cleaner attract customers and show expertise.

They link naturally to service pages.

Reviews and recurring customers

Recurring clients are ideal reviewers

Ask after the first few visits, when satisfaction is clear, and reply to every review. Reviews mentioning specific services and areas help both customers and relevance.

Cleaning services SEO in different countries

Terms vary: end of tenancy cleaning in the UK, bond cleaning or end of lease cleaning in Australia, move out cleaning in the US and Canada, maid service and deep cleaning Dubai in the UAE.

Pages use the language your customers use.

Service-area pages that are not copies

Cleaning companies often serve many neighbourhoods

Instead of copying one page with a different place name, build pages only for areas where you have regular clients, and include what is specific: typical property types, parking or access notes, how often your teams are in the area, local reviews and photos of local jobs. Fewer, stronger pages outperform dozens of near-duplicates and stay within Google's guidance on doorway pages.

A typical first three months

Month one covers the audit, profile clean-up, tracking and the main service pages.

Month two adds commercial and specialist pages, trust content and a review routine.

Month three adds priority area pages, helpful guides and local links from community groups, property managers and suppliers.

Each month's report focuses on quotes and bookings.

Recruiting cleaners through search

Many cleaning companies struggle to hire

A careers page explaining pay structure, hours, training and how to apply can rank for local cleaning jobs searches, helping growth keep pace with demand.

Cleaning services SEO for franchises and multi-branch firms

Companies with several branches need a page and profile for each real branch, consistent service names across them and local reviews for each.

Central templates keep quality consistent while local teams add photos and area details.
