How to read these SEO case studies

Each case study explains the starting point, the challenge, the strategy across technical SEO, content, AI search and links, the work delivered and the results, with screenshots as the source.

Numbers are rounded rather than presented with false precision, and each figure is dated. Search performance moves constantly, so a figure is a snapshot, not a promise for another site.

The two projects are deliberately different

SpicyRanked is my own review platform, built on a young domain, and shows how growth can be created from a low base. FeetFinder is a large creator marketplace, where the job is protecting and compounding traffic at scale.

What these SEO case studies have in common

Technical foundations first: crawlable templates, clean indexing and internal links

Content built around search intent and real reader questions

Answer-ready structure for AI Overviews and assistants

Authority earned without buying links

Why there are not more case studies here

Many client projects are covered by confidentiality agreements, especially white label work for agencies.

I only publish case studies where I can show proof and have permission, rather than anonymous claims that cannot be checked.
