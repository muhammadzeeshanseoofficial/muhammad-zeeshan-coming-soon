What affects SEO cost

  • Competition: a plumber in a small town needs less work than an online store competing nationally.
  • Site size and platform: a 20-page WordPress site and a 50,000-URL catalogue need very different technical work.
  • Starting point: sites with indexing problems, penalties or a history of risky links need more remediation.
  • Content needs: the number of new pages and refreshes each month is often the biggest cost driver.
  • Markets and languages: each additional country or language adds research, content and technical setup.
  • Who implements: if your team implements changes, the cost is lower than if I do everything.

Because of these factors, fixed prices are given after a short review of your site rather than as one figure for everyone.

Monthly packages versus one-off projects

Monthly packages suit businesses that want ongoing improvement: technical monitoring, on-page updates, content and authority building. One-off projects suit specific needs, such as an audit, a migration, a technical fix sprint or local SEO setup. Many clients start with an audit, then decide whether a monthly package makes sense.

Why cheap SEO often costs more

Very cheap packages usually rely on automated reports, bulk links or mass-produced pages. These can trigger Google's spam systems, and fixing the damage later costs more than doing the work properly the first time. When comparing quotes, ask exactly what will be done each month, who will do it and how results will be measured.
