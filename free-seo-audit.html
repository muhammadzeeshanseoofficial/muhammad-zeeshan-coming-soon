Most free audits are automated reports with hundreds of warnings and a score.

This one is a short, written review by a person.

Free Website Audit vs the Full SEO Audit

The free audit is deliberately focused: key pages, the most visible issues and quick wins.

The paid SEO audit services cover the whole site in depth, including technical health across every template, content and intent mapping, internal links, backlinks and competitors, with a prioritised roadmap and walkthrough call.

Many clients start with the free review and decide afterwards whether a full audit is worthwhile.

Who the Free Audit Is for

Health, medical, dental, legal, finance and insurance sites are not covered. Agencies can request a review of a prospect's site as part of white label pre-sales support.
